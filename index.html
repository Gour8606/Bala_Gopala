🕉️ Guru Sandipani's 16 Kalas & 64 Vidhyas

👶 Toddlers Born April 2024 (~2.5 - 3 Years Old)

In Srimad Bhagavatam (10.45.27–36), Lord Sri Krishna and Balarama studied at Maharishi Sandipani's Gurukul in Avantika (Ujjain). Krishna is celebrated as the supreme Purna Avatara who expressed all 16 Kalas (Shodasha Kalas) and mastered the 64 Vidhyas (Chatushashti Kalas) in 64 days!

🌟 The 16 Divine Kalas (Shodasha Kalas):

  • 1. Shri (Grace & Beauty): Aesthetic harmony and joyful auspicious surroundings.
  • 2. Bhu (Grounding / Earth): Tactile sensory play with natural elements.
  • 3. Kirti (Glory): Celebrating positive effort without fear of failure.
  • 4. Ila (Divine Speech): Clear articulation through Sanskrit syllables (Om, Radhe, Govinda).
  • 5. Lila (Playfulness): Free, unhurried, joyful exploration (Bala Leela).
  • 6. Kanti (Luster): Visual stimulation with sacred colors (Saffron, Peacock Teal, Lotus Pink).
  • 7. Vidya (Knowledge): Shape matching and pattern recognition.
  • 8. Vimala (Purity): Compassion towards living beings (Gau-Seva & Ahimsa).
  • 9. Utkarshana (Inspiration): Lifting natural curiosity through cause-and-effect.
  • 10. Jnana (Wisdom): Intuitive feeling of the sacred in all nature.
  • 11. Kriya (Dexterity): Motor coordination (pincer tap, circular tracing, drag-and-drop).
  • 12. Yoga (Harmonious Union): Synchronization of eye, hand, and musical ear.
  • 13. Prahvi (Humility & Reverence): Offering flowers (Pushparchana) at the altar.
  • 14. Satya (Truthfulness): Nurturing natural childhood innocence.
  • 15. Isana (Mastery): Gaining confidence in rhythm and self-expression.
  • 16. Anugraha (Divine Grace): Feeling cherished and protected by the Divine.

🪈 The 64 Vidhyas Represented in This App:

  • Gita & Vadya Vidya (Arts 1 & 2): Bansuri flute, Temple Bell (Ghanti), Shankha, and the 7 Vedic animal-rooted Swaras (Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni Sa).
  • Udaka-Vadya (Art 12): The ancient art of playing tuned water bowls (Jal Tarang).
  • Pushpastarana & Malya-Grathana (Arts 8 & 15): Offering pink lotus, marigold, and tulsi garlands.
  • Alekhya & Visheshakacchedya (Arts 4 & 5): Drawing sacred rangoli with golden diya light and sacred stamps (Om, Diya, Charan).
  • Shuka-Sarika-Pralapana & Gau-Seva (Arts 42 & 43): Bathing, feeding, and caring for holy Gau-Mata, calf, parrot, deer, and monkey.
  • Bhushana-Yojana & Veshya (Art 44): Sacred Shringar adornment of Bala Gopala with peacock feather, flute, garland, and sweet butter!
  • Balakridanaka (Art 46): Popping sweet butter pots and catching fluttering peacock feathers!
